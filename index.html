See the ITGC engagement LinkedIn Résumé (PDF)

A clean SOC report does not mean a controlled client. Assurance is only as strong as the half of the control the client owns.

From 2011 to 2019 I led e-business operations at First Bank of Nigeria, a bank with more than 11 million cards issued and about 550 branches. Throughout, I was the bank's main contact with Visa and Mastercard on PCI DSS and ISO 27001 audit findings.

I did not perform those audits. My job was to get the findings closed: coordinating remediation of several hundred findings across IT, information security, facilities and HR, and reporting closure to senior management.

Now I work from the auditor's side. My focus is ITGC testing, service organization (SOC 1) reliance and third-party risk for Canadian financial institutions and the firms that audit them.

I know what a finding needs for a client to act on it: a clear condition, a real cause, an owner and a practical fix.
